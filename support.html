📋 Frequently Asked Questions

💰 How do I deposit?

Go to Deposit page → View bank details → Send money → Contact admin on WhatsApp for approval.

💸 How do I withdraw?

Go to Withdraw page → Enter bank details and amount → Submit request → Admin processes it.

📋 How do I earn money?

Complete daily tasks on the Tasks page. You need to be on Rox 1 or higher to earn.

🤝 How does the referral system work?

Share your referral code → Friend registers → You get 10% of their first deposit → Friend gets ₦200 bonus.
